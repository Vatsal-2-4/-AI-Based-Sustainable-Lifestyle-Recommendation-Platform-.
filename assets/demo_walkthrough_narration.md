# Evergreen walkthrough narration

The accompanying animation is a guided visual tour rather than a direct recording of a live session. The screens marked **SAMPLE UI** use illustrative values. Read this script alongside the animation; it follows the scenes in order and takes about 30 seconds.

| Approx. time | Narration |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:04 | “Welcome to Evergreen. It helps you understand parts of your everyday footprint and find practical actions that fit your routine.” |
| 0:04–0:07 | “You can explore in demo mode or create an account to keep your profile, estimates and goals together.” |
| 0:07–0:10 | “The lifestyle questionnaire collects commute, food, home energy, waste and shopping details to personalize the experience.” |
| 0:10–0:13 | “The dashboard brings saved estimates, goals and recent activity into one view, with an onboarding path for first-time users.” |
| 0:13–0:17 | “The calculator annualizes the activity you enter. It shows transport, electricity and food emissions, while water and waste stay separate resource indicators.” |
| 0:17–0:20 | “Recommendations are ranked using profile text, TF-IDF and cosine similarity. Each action explains which habits matched, and can be saved as a goal.” |
| 0:20–0:24 | “Progress tracking lets you update, pause or complete goals, and compare estimates when their factors and included inputs are compatible.” |
| 0:24–0:27 | “The profile page lets you review account details, export your data as JSON, or download an account-only SQLite backup.” |
| 0:27–0:30 | “This is an academic prototype. Its footprint is partial, and it does not claim a verified sustainability score or measured savings from actions.” |

Before presenting, start the app and use the **Demo User** account so you can demonstrate the live questionnaire, calculator, recommendation matching and goal controls after the visual tour.
